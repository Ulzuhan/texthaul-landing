Privacy Policy

TextHaul keeps your capture data local.

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Short version:

TextHaul does not collect, transmit, or store your screenshots or extracted text on external servers. OCR runs locally in your browser, and your capture library stays on your device.

1. Data stored locally

TextHaul stores the following information locally in your browser so the extension can work:

  • Captured screenshots and thumbnail previews
  • Extracted OCR text, optional edited text, and OCR confidence metadata
  • Page title, source URL, tags, favorites, and review status
  • Extension settings such as OCR language, interface language, retention, theme, and copy preferences

Captures are stored in IndexedDB. Settings are stored locally in extension storage. You can delete captures from the extension and uninstalling TextHaul removes its locally stored data from the browser.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No browsing history collection
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies
  • No remote OCR uploads
  • No sale or transfer of capture data to third parties

3. OCR processing

OCR runs inside the browser using locally bundled Tesseract assets. Screenshots are not sent to an external OCR API, and the extension does not load executable code from remote servers at runtime.

4. Permissions

TextHaul requests only the Chrome permissions needed for its visible features. The production extension requests no persistent host permissions.

5. Limited Use disclosure

TextHaul’s handling of user data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. TextHaul uses user data only to provide its single purpose: capture visible webpage content at the user’s request, run local OCR, and let the user review, organize, and export the result.

6. Data retention and deletion

You control local retention from the extension settings. You can delete individual captures, clear all captures, or uninstall TextHaul to remove locally stored extension data from the browser.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the Chrome Web Store page once the listing is live.
