Your data belongs to you

TextHaul is built on a simple principle: your screenshots and extracted text are yours alone. We don't want them, we don't need them, and we never see them.

  • No cloud processing

    OCR runs entirely in a Web Worker inside your browser using locally bundled language models.

  • No accounts or tracking

    No email required, no analytics, no cookies. Just install and use.

  • Local storage only

    All captures are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Clear them anytime from the extension settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TextHaul.

Is TextHaul really free?
Yes, TextHaul is completely free. There are no premium tiers, no subscriptions, and no hidden costs. The extension is open-source and will remain free forever.
Does it work offline?
Absolutely. All language models and the OCR engine are bundled with the extension. Once installed, TextHaul works entirely offline with no internet connection required.
How accurate is the OCR?
TextHaul uses Tesseract.js 5.1, the industry-standard open-source OCR engine. Accuracy is excellent for printed text and good for most handwriting. Results vary with image quality and text complexity.
Where is my data stored?
All your captures, text, and thumbnails are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB database. Nothing is uploaded to any server. You can clear all data or set an automatic cleanup period in the extension settings.
Which languages are supported?
English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Hindi. More languages can be added in future updates.
What browsers are supported?
TextHaul requires Chrome 105+ or any Chromium-based browser (Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera) that supports Manifest V3 and the scripting API. Firefox support is not available at this time.
